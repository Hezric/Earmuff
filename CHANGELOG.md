### Features

- Protect the default Windows audio output with per-device switches, configurable limits and automatic full-volume reduction.
- Manage protection and timed pauses from an English or Simplified Chinese flyout.
- Share the native shell, UI and build tooling through the signed Linen v0.1.1 release.
- Show full-contrast headphone marks for protected, unprotected and paused states.

### Fixes

- Open with the correct theme background, native rounded corners and DWM shadow.
- Toggle on tray clicks, dismiss on Escape or outside clicks, and release the webview after closing.
- Dock hidden-tray flyouts beside the notification area with matching entry and exit motion.
